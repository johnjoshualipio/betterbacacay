# BetterBacacay content research

Audience: Bacacay residents, visitors, students, researchers, and civic contributors.

Accessed: 2026-09-07

## Scope and assumptions

This research supports static public pages for Bacacay, Albay. Claims are limited to
municipal profile data, publicly documented tourism and culture references, public-service
starting points, emergency references, and civic-source context. Current officials,
fees, schedules, routes, facilities, and emergency contacts are not published unless a
current primary source supports them.

## Direct answer

Bacacay is a first-class municipality in Albay with 56 barangays and a 2024 population of
72,298 according to the Philippine Statistics Authority. Provincial tourism material
places Bacacay in Albay's East Coast circuit and identifies Cagraray Eco Park, Sula
Channel, Mataas, Pinamuntugan Island, black-sand beaches, and Mt. Manaet as part of the
area's tourism story. PCAARRD documents karagumoy production in Cagraray Island and its
handicraft and livelihood context. DILG Region 5 documents a 2024 Project A.S.S.I.S.T.
activity in Bacacay. The Bacacay Water District publishes a public-service reference and
contact starting point.

## Limitations

Provincial tourism pages were readable in search results but returned access restrictions
when opened directly. Their URLs are still linked as the first-party reference, and the
site avoids copying unsupported operating hours, prices, coordinates, or access claims.
Current elected officials were not added because the accessible DILG masterlist found in
research covers the 2022–2025 term; the site links the DILG directory instead.

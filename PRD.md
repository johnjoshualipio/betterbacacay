# BetterBacacay — Product Requirements Document

**Product:** BetterBacacay  
**Planned Domain:** `betterbacacay.org`  
**Location:** Municipality of Bacacay, Albay, Philippines  
**Project Type:** Community-driven civic information and transparency portal  
**Ecosystem:** BetterGov.ph / BetterLGU  
**Initial Directory Status:** 🔵 Planned  
**Primary Audience:** Bacacay residents, visitors, local businesses, students, researchers, civic groups, and anyone looking for reliable local information  
**Document Status:** Initial Product Requirements Document  

---

## 1. Product Summary

BetterBacacay is an independent, community-driven digital portal for the Municipality of Bacacay, Albay.

Its purpose is to make useful local information easier to find, understand, and access in one place, including:

- Local government information
- Public services and procedures
- Barangay information
- Officials and offices
- Ordinances and resolutions
- Budgets, projects, and transparency data
- Emergency contacts
- Tourism destinations
- Festivals and local culture
- Community announcements
- Local opportunities
- Downloadable public forms and references

BetterBacacay will participate in the **BetterGov.ph BetterLGU ecosystem**, which encourages civic-minded developers and volunteers to build public transparency portals for Philippine local government units.

The portal should be clearly presented as a **community-maintained civic project**, not as the official website of the Municipality of Bacacay unless the project later receives formal adoption or endorsement.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Create the most useful public digital guide to Bacacay — a single, accessible place where residents and visitors can understand the municipality, find public information, discover services, explore its communities, and appreciate what makes Bacacay distinct.

BetterBacacay should feel like a digital extension of Bacacay itself:

> **Woven by tradition, connected by the sea, and built for the community.**

---

# 3. Mission

BetterBacacay exists to:

1. Make local public information easier to discover.
2. Improve transparency and civic awareness.
3. Organize fragmented government information into understandable pages.
4. Make public services easier to navigate.
5. Give every barangay a discoverable digital presence.
6. Promote Bacacay's islands, culture, history, and local livelihoods.
7. Encourage community participation without pretending to replace official LGU channels.
8. Preserve useful local information in an open and maintainable format.

# 5. Problem Statement

Information about a municipality is often scattered across:

- Official LGU social media pages
- Individual office pages
- Facebook announcements
- Government documents
- PDF files
- National agency websites
- Barangay pages
- Tourism posts
- News sites
- Search results
- Informal community pages

This creates several problems:

- Residents may not know which office handles a service.
- Public requirements and procedures may be difficult to find.
- Emergency numbers are not always available in one location.
- Ordinances, projects, and budget information are difficult for ordinary citizens to browse.
- Barangay information is fragmented.
- Tourism information lacks a consistent municipal guide.
- Older announcements become difficult to rediscover.
- Local cultural information may disappear or be poorly documented.
- Residents may depend heavily on Facebook posts for public information.

BetterBacacay addresses these problems by organizing public and civic information into a searchable, structured, permanent web portal.

# 6. Product Principles

## 6.1 Useful Before Decorative

Every page should help the user complete a task, understand something, or discover information.

Decorative content must never overpower public information.

---

## 6.2 Local Before Generic

The product should visibly belong to Bacacay.

Its identity should come from:

- Bacacay's coastline
- Islands
- Cagraray
- Sula Channel
- Karagumoy weaving
- Fishing communities
- Local history
- Barangays
- Festivals
- Local livelihoods

Do not build the identity primarily around generic Philippine government visuals.

---

## 6.3 Independent but Respectful

BetterBacacay should never imply official authority it does not have.

The portal should clearly distinguish:

- BetterBacacay-maintained information
- Official LGU information
- External government sources
- Community-submitted information

---

## 6.4 Source Everything Important

Public-information pages should display sources whenever practical.

Examples:

- Official LGU post
- Municipal ordinance
- COA document
- DBM publication
- PSA data
- DILG source
- Tourism office material
- Government agency source

Each content record should ideally include:

- Source title
- Source URL
- Publishing agency
- Publication date
- Last verified date

---

## 6.5 Mobile First

Many residents will access the portal using a phone.

All essential features must work well on:

- Mobile browsers
- Slow connections
- Small screens
- Lower-end devices

---

## 6.6 Accessible to Ordinary Citizens

Avoid bureaucratic language when a simpler explanation is possible.

Official terminology may be retained where necessary, but should be explained.

# 7. Goals

## Primary Goals

### G1 — Centralize Local Information

Create one searchable location for Bacacay public information.

### G2 — Improve Service Discoverability

Help residents answer:

- Where do I go?
- What do I need?
- Who do I contact?
- What are the office hours?
- Is there a form?
- Is there an official online service?

### G3 — Improve Transparency

Make civic information understandable and browsable.

### G4 — Digitally Represent Every Barangay

Create a public profile for all **56 barangays**.

### G5 — Promote Bacacay

Build a high-quality tourism and culture guide centered on Bacacay's own identity.

### G6 — Encourage Civic Participation

Give users clear ways to:

- Send suggestions
- Report outdated information
- Contribute corrections
- Volunteer
- Participate in community initiatives

### G7 — Stay Maintainable

Content should be structured so contributors can update the portal without redesigning pages.

---

# 8. Non-Goals

The initial product will **not** attempt to:

- Replace the official LGU website
- Represent itself as the official Municipality of Bacacay portal
- Issue permits or licenses
- Accept government payments
- Process taxes
- Store government case records
- Replace emergency services
- Replace official hotlines
- Maintain authoritative resident records
- Provide legal determinations
- Provide confidential government transactions
- Require residents to create accounts for basic information
- Build a social network
- Add unnecessary gamification
- Use AI features without a clear public-service use case

Where an official online transaction exists, BetterBacacay should link the user to the official service.

# 52. Error and Empty States

Empty sections should never contain fake content.

Examples:

Instead of fake ordinances:

> Ordinance records are currently being compiled. View available public sources or help contribute verified documents.

Instead of fake projects:

> We are still gathering verified project information.

Always provide a useful next action.

# 53. Content Integrity Rules

Never:

- Invent officials
- Invent phone numbers
- Invent government email addresses
- Invent office hours
- Invent tourism fees
- Invent ordinances
- Invent projects
- Invent budgets
- Invent event dates
- Invent social media accounts
- Generate fake historical claims
- Present AI-generated images as documentary evidence

Unknown information should be clearly marked as unavailable or awaiting verification.

# 54. Image Policy

Prefer:

1. Official/publicly licensed Bacacay images
2. Contributor photography with permission
3. Government media with clear reuse permission
4. Open-license media

AI-generated imagery may be used for:

- Concept artwork

It should not be used as evidence of:

- Actual destinations
- Actual public projects
- Actual officials
- Actual events

Public information is structured, sourced, dated, and understandable.

### Complete local coverage

All 56 barangays have a place within the portal.

### Tourism and culture

Bacacay's tourism identity is treated as first-class content.

### Distinct visual identity

The interface draws from:

**Sea + islands + karagumoy weaving + community**

### Community maintainability

The portal can continue improving through open civic collaboration.

# 63. Final Product Standard

A feature belongs in BetterBacacay only if it improves at least one of the following:

- Access to public information
- Access to services
- Transparency
- Safety
- Community participation
- Local knowledge
- Tourism discovery
- Preservation of Bacacay's identity

If a feature only makes the portal look more sophisticated without improving public usefulness, it should not be prioritized.

BetterBacacay should remain:

**simple, factual, local, open, useful, and maintainable.**
